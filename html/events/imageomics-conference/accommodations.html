Lodging & Other Considerations

Hotel Accommodations:

The recommended hotels for the Imageomics Institute Conference are the Homewood Suites by Hilton Columbus/OSU and TownePlace Suites by Marriott Columbus North – OSU. For your convenience, these hotels are located adjacent to the OSU West Campus and are approximately a 20-minute walk to the Energy Advancement and Innovation Center (EAIC), which serves as the location of the meeting.

Making Reservations:

A block of rooms at each hotel is being held under OSU – Imageomics on a first-come, first-served basis through March 14. After this date, rooms will be released to the public. If a preferred room type is unavailable under the group block, you will need to book separately from the room block and will be subject to the prevailing room rate available at the hotel.

For reservations within the block, reserve online using the dedicated group reservation sites below, adjusting the check-in and check-out dates as needed, or call the sales department at 614-488-1500 ext. 606 and mention Imageomics when making your reservation to receive our special discounted group rate.

  • Homewood Suites for 1 King Studio Rooms
  • TownePlace Suites for 2 Queen Studio Rooms

If you are paying with a P-card: Your name must either be on the P-card used to make the reservation or you must provide a credit card authorization form prior to your arrival. Otherwise, you will be required to provide a separate credit card, such as a personal credit card, upon check-in.

If there are no rooms left at Homewood Suites or TownePlace Suites, we recommend the Blackwell Inn. This hotel is located on the OSU campus and is approximately a 20-minute walk to the EAIC.

Physical, Religious or Other Accommodations:

We recognize that some of our guests may require physical, dietary, or other accommodations to fully participate in the conference. If you need any type of accommodation to participate, please reach out to Program Manager Brittany Fonner. We will make every effort to ensure you receive whatever accommodations you need to support your participation.
