Team Science Resources

The Imageomics Institute is committed to advancing the field of team science.

Our dedicated team scientists are testing known frameworks in practical applications to help our institute form more effective teams. They are also sharing their discoveries and insights through publications and conferences for team scientists across the globe. By training the next generation of team scientists, we aim to create more effective and adaptable science teams, ensuring the continual advancement of team science. 

Open Source Team Science Tools & Resources

Apprentice Facilitator Guide 

The Apprentice Facilitators Circle serves the dual purpose of professional development for NextGens and capacity building for the Institute. 

Facilitation refers to the act of designing, moderating and enabling team interactive dynamics.  Every interaction among team members, if designed and implemented properly, can become an opportunity for convergence. Facilitation knowledge and skills open up abundant opportunities for every team member to influence the convergence process. View Guide

Ideation Handbook

Ideation is a very important part of scientific discoveries. To quote Dr. Dan Rubenstein, “Science is about ideas and good science emerges from good ideas and good ideas come from collective thinking.” Practically, a well facilitated ideation process serves three main purposes: 1) Generate and consider new ideas; 2. It checks in with the overall research vision of the Institute; 3. It enhances coherence among sub-teams. View Handbook
