Save the Date!

Mark your calendars for the 2027 Imageomics Conference, taking place May 24–26, 2027, at The Ohio State University in Columbus, Ohio!

Co-hosted by the Imageomics Institute and the National Synthesis Center for Organismal Resilience (NSCORE), the conference will bring together researchers, practitioners, and students for three days of connection, collaboration, and community at the intersection of AI, computer vision, and biodiversity science.

More details about the conference program, registration, and opportunities to participate are coming soon.
