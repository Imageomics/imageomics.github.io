NextGen Day Agenda

Tuesday, April 14 | NextGen Day | Energy Advancement and Innovation Center

All times listed are in Eastern Time.

Room 130, Energy Advancement and Innovation Center

8:30 AM | Registration

9:00 AM | Welcome by NextGen Day Organizing Committee

Caleb Charpentier (Virginia Tech), Braden DeMattei (The Ohio State University), Rhoda Nankabirwa (University of Maryland Baltimore County), and Alabi Ahmed (University of Maryland Baltimore County)

9:10 AM | Icebreaker Activity: What the Jargon?

Ever feel like every discipline comes with its own secret handshake? Welcome to What the Jargon?—the icebreaker where we decode the academic alphabet soup together. This session explores the “chameleon terms” that shift meaning as they cross disciplinary lines. Through quick conversations and collaborative sleuthing, you’ll discover how the same term can mean totally different things depending on who’s in the room.

10:10 AM | Illuminating Concepts Through Tools

Caleb Charpentier (Virginia Tech) and Braden DeMattei (The Ohio State University)

Big ideas land better when you can see them work. This session uses practical tools to make complex concepts in biology and computer science tangible.

10:40 AM | Break

11:00 AM | Career Roundtables: Interdisciplinary Career Pathways in Bio + Computer Science

Join informal, small-group conversations with fellow NextGens and faculty to talk candidly about navigating careers at the intersection of biology and computing. Ask questions, swap insights, and gather practical advice on industry paths, job searches, and building a network that works for you.

Table Hosts: Chenyang Wei (The Ohio State University); François Leroy (The Ohio State University); Sam Stevens (The Ohio State University); Jianyang Gu (The Ohio State University); and Wei-Lun (Harry) Chao (Boston University).

12:00 PM | Catered Lunch

1:00 PM | Navigating the Borderlands: Thriving in Interdisciplinary Science

Gather for an open circle discussion about the challenges and rewards of working between fields. Share experiences, swap strategies, and explore how to build confidence, community, and clarity while forging a path across disciplines.

2:00 PM | Dissertations 101: Designing the Question, Surviving the Process

How do strong dissertation questions take shape—and how do you navigate the process without feeling alone? This session pairs early-stage students with those further along to share real experiences, practical strategies, and the human side of the journey. Together, we’ll demystify question development, surface hidden resources, and build connections that make the path forward clearer (and far less isolating).

2:45 PM | Break

3:15 PM | Sharing Your Science: Communicating Research with Confidence

Craft a clear, compelling pitch for your research—then pressure-test it. Participants will draft a short research pitch, refine it using the Simple Writer tool Up-Goer Five, and practice “research speed dating” with peers.

You’ll experiment with tailoring your message: leaning into discipline-specific language when speaking to insiders and translating into plain language when reaching broader audiences. The goal? Communicate your work with clarity, confidence, and impact—no matter who’s listening.

4:00 PM | Open Space

5:00 PM | Adjourn

6:00 PM | Welcome Dinner @ Tanya & Mosheh's Home
