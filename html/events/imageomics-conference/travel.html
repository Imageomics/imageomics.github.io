First Imageomics Conference

Travel Information

Booking Airfare:

Air travelers should arrive and depart from John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH).

CMH is approximately 15 minutes from campus and lodging.

Dates of Attendance:

Tuesday, April 14 – Pre-Conference Activities: open to all students/postdocs including the broader imageomics community and imageomics grant personnel.

Pre-conference activities will include NextGen-only Day1 for students and postdocs and a separate activity2 (TBD) for imageomics faculty and staff.

1NextGen Day is open to all students and postdocs within the broader imageomics community.

2The faculty/staff activity is exclusive to past and present imageomics grant personnel (faculty, staff, and research scientists) and may have limited capacity. Additional details on the exact activity will be shared once confirmed.

Wednesday, April 15 & Thursday, April 16 – Imageomics Conference: Open to the broader imageomics community.

Thursday, April 16 – Friday, April 17 – Imageomics Tooling Workshop “Add-on”: Open to the broader imageomics community.

Arrival and Departure Dates:

For those participating in pre-conference activities, please plan to arrive by the evening of April 13.

For those attending the Tooling workshop, we recommend you book return travel no earlier than 4 p.m. ET on April 17.

Location:

The meeting will take place on The Ohio State University campus in room 130 at the Energy Advancement and Innovation Center, located at 2281 Kenny Rd., Columbus, OH 43210.

Getting to Campus:

For convenience, we recommend taking a taxi or using a ridesharing company to and from the airport, as most locations (meeting site and hotel) are within walking distance.

If you are arriving by car, the closest visitor parking to the EAIC building is the surface lot located behind the building off of Carmenton Ave and Innovation Way.

If this lot is full, the next closest visitor parking lot is Carmack 5.
