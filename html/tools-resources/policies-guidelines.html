Imageomics Policies & Guidelines

Imageomics Institute’s guidelines and policies have been created to support responsible, collaborative, and effective research. They outline key expectations, procedures, and best practices that guide the institute’s activities, helping ensure transparency, consistency, and a shared understanding among researchers, students, and partners. These resources are intended to support a productive and inclusive research environment while enabling the Imageomics community to work together efficiently and ethically.
